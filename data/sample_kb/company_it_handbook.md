1. COMPANY IT HANDBOOK
Purpose

The Company IT Handbook provides employees with guidelines, standards, and expectations for the proper use of company technology resources. It ensures security, compliance, productivity, and consistency across the organization.

Chapter 1: Acceptable Use of IT Resources
Overview

Company-issued technology assets are provided to support business operations and employee productivity.

Covered Assets
Laptops and desktops
Mobile devices
Email systems
Collaboration tools
VPN access
Cloud storage
Business applications
Shared network drives
Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

Use IT resources for business purposes.
Maintain the confidentiality of company information.
Report security incidents immediately.
Follow software licensing requirements.
Protect company-issued equipment.
Prohibited Activities

Employees must not:

Install unauthorized software.
Share corporate passwords.
Access illegal or inappropriate content.
Store confidential company information on personal devices without authorization.
Attempt to bypass security controls.
Chapter 2: Information Security
Password Policy

Passwords must:

Be at least 12 characters long.
Include uppercase and lowercase letters.
Include numbers and special characters.
Not be reused across systems.
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

All employees must use MFA for:

Email access
VPN connections
Cloud applications
Administrative systems
Chapter 3: Email Usage Guidelines
Email Best Practices

Employees should:

Use professional communication.
Verify recipients before sending.
Avoid sharing confidential information without encryption.
Report suspicious emails.
Phishing Awareness

Common phishing indicators:

Urgent requests for action.
Unexpected attachments.
Misspelled domains.
Requests for passwords.
Reporting Suspicious Emails
Do not click links.
Do not open attachments.
Report to Service Desk.
Delete the email after confirmation.
Chapter 4: Laptop and Device Management
Company Laptop Usage

Employees must:

Keep operating systems updated.
Lock devices when unattended.
Use approved antivirus software.
Store devices securely.
Lost or Stolen Equipment

Immediately report:

Device serial number
Last known location
Approximate time of loss

IT will:

Initiate device tracking
Revoke access credentials
Perform remote wipe if applicable
Chapter 5: Remote Work Guidelines
Remote Access

Approved methods include:

Corporate VPN
Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI)
Company-managed devices
Home Network Recommendations
Secure Wi-Fi with WPA2/WPA3
Updated router firmware
Strong Wi-Fi passwords
Chapter 6: Software Installation Policy

Employees may only install:

Approved company applications
IT-authorized productivity tools

All software requests must be submitted through:

Service Desk Portal
IT Request Forms
Chapter 7: Data Classification
Classification Levels
Public

Information approved for public distribution.

Internal

Business information for internal use.

Confidential

Sensitive business data requiring restricted access.

Restricted

Highly sensitive information requiring strict controls.

Examples include:

Customer information
Payroll records
Financial statements
Legal documents
Chapter 8: Incident Reporting

Employees must report:

Security incidents
Malware infections
Lost devices
Unauthorized access attempts
Data leakage concerns
Reporting Process
Contact Service Desk.
Provide incident details.
Preserve evidence.
Await further instructions.
Chapter 9: Employee Offboarding

When an employee leaves:

IT will:

Disable accounts.
Retrieve assets.
Revoke application access.
Archive required data.
Update asset inventory.
